# OpenTailcat 1.2.13

Development build. Not a production privacy VPN. Do not claim leak-free.

## Highlights

- **Fix intermittent `VPN data plane failed: no fresh engine health for 6s`
  auto-close.** Native `rateCalcLoop` already refreshes `healthUnixSec` about
  every 1s (pumps-alive heartbeat, independent of DiscoPing). Kotlin was
  tearing the tunnel down on a **single** poll once wall-clock age exceeded
  **5s** (`NetworkMetrics.isLiveRunning`). Brief process suspension, GC, or
  binder stall could age the last sample to 6s while transport and pumps were
  still live.
- Freshness window widened to **15s** (DiscoPing interval 5s + timeout 2s +
  poll/margin). Rejects health timestamps more than **2s** in the future
  (AUDIT medium finding).
- **HealthStale** teardown now requires **3 consecutive** stale polls (~3s
  after the window already expired). `PumpFailed` and `TransportLost` still
  fail closed on the first observation.
- Native `GetStatsJSON` re-loads `healthUnixSec` after `bridge.GetStats()` so a
  slow stats snapshot cannot publish an artificially aged heartbeat. AAR
  rebuilt with Go 1.27.1 + NDK 29.0.14206865 for this release.

## Not claimed

Phase 8 physical dual-capture / leak-free / production signing. Always-on
lockdown still required on Android 10+. Development-keystore signing when
`OPENTAILCAT_RELEASE_*` is unset.
